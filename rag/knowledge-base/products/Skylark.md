# Skylark

Fretwork's parlour acoustic, introduced in 2023. The small guitar in the range:
designed by Jun Watanabe to be comfortable played sitting down, and the one
Fretwork recommends as a first acoustic or a travelling one.

## Specifications

- Solid mahogany top, back, and sides.
- Parlour body, 24.75" scale, 1.72" nut width.
- Bone nut and saddle, rosewood bridge, open-gear tuners.
- Available as Skylark (acoustic) and Skylark E (undersaddle pickup and preamp
  with a built-in tuner).

## Pricing and options

- Skylark: £699. Skylark E: £849.
- Finishes: Natural satin, Ink.
- Ships in a fitted gig bag; hard case +£110.

## Notes

- Built by Kestrel Lutherie, like the Driftwood; acoustic build-to-ship dates
  run a week longer in winter.
- Strung with .011 phosphor bronze as standard.
- Quieter and more mid-focused than the Driftwood — the Driftwood remains the
  fingerstyle recommendation; the Skylark is the one that gets picked up.
- Covered by the 30-day play-it-at-home returns policy.
