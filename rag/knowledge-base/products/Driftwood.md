# Driftwood

Fretwork's acoustic guitar line, introduced in 2020. A fingerstyle-leaning
instrument rather than a strummer.

## Specifications

- Solid Sitka spruce top; mahogany back and sides.
- Grand Concert body, 25.4" scale, 1.75" nut width.
- Bone nut and saddle, ebony bridge.
- Available in Driftwood (acoustic) and Driftwood E (undersaddle pickup and
  preamp with a built-in tuner).

## Pricing and options

- Driftwood: £999. Driftwood E: £1,149.
- Finishes: Natural satin, Tobacco burst.
- Ships in a hard case as standard.

## Notes

- The narrower Grand Concert body suits smaller players and seated playing; it
  is the instrument Fretwork recommends for fingerstyle over the Meridian.
- Strung with .012 phosphor bronze; .011 available at checkout.
- Covered by the 30-day play-it-at-home returns policy.
