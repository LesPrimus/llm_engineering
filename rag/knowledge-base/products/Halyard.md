# Halyard

Fretwork's semi-hollow electric, introduced in 2024 and the newest line. Aimed
at the player who already owns a Meridian and wants a different voice rather
than a first electric.

## Specifications

- Laminated maple semi-hollow body with a centre block, 16" wide.
- Set mahogany neck, 24.75" scale, rolled fingerboard edges.
- 22 medium-jumbo frets, 12" fingerboard radius.
- Two humbuckers, three-way switch, two volumes and two tones.
- Tune-o-matic bridge with a stop bar; Bigsby vibrato built to order.

## Pricing and options

- Halyard: £1,199. With Bigsby: £1,349, adds one week to the build-to-ship date.
- Finishes: Oxblood, Midnight, Natural.
- Ships in a hard case as standard.

## Notes

- Built by Halverson Works, like the Meridian and the Lowtide.
- Set up with .010 strings as standard; .011 available at checkout.
- Feeds back before the Meridian does at high gain — the centre block is there
  to push that further out, not to remove it.
- Covered by the 30-day play-it-at-home returns policy.
