# Meridian

Fretwork's electric guitar line, and the first instrument the company shipped
(2019). Aimed at the player moving on from a starter guitar.

## Specifications

- Double-cutaway alder body, bolt-on roasted maple neck, 25.5" scale.
- 22 medium-jumbo frets, 12" fingerboard radius.
- Two-point vibrato bridge with a steel block.
- Available in Meridian S (three single coils) and Meridian HS (humbucker in the
  bridge).

## Pricing and options

- Meridian S: £849. Meridian HS: £899.
- Finishes: Sea Foam, Butterscotch, Midnight, Natural.
- Left-handed built to order, +£60, adds one week to the build-to-ship date.
- Ships in a fitted gig bag; hard case +£120.

## Notes

- Set up on the Bristol bench for the string gauge chosen at checkout (.009 or
  .010 standard).
- Best-selling line; roughly half of Fretwork's instrument revenue.
- Covered by the 30-day play-it-at-home returns policy.
