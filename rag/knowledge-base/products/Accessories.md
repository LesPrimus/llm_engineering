# Accessories

The small range Fretwork sells alongside the instruments. All of it ships from
warehouse stock rather than being built to order, so an accessories-only order
does not wait on the bench queue.

## What is sold

- Hard cases: £120 for the Meridian, £140 for the Lowtide, £110 for the Skylark.
  The Driftwood and the Halyard already ship in one.
- Gig bags: £45, padded, the same bag the Meridian and Lowtide ship in.
- Strings: £8.50 a set, the gauges the bench sets up with — .009 and .010
  electric, .045-.105 bass, .011 and .012 phosphor bronze acoustic.
- Straps: £35 leather, £22 woven.
- Roundhouse footswitch: £45. Spare valve set: £60.
- Setup kit: £75 — feeler gauges, string action gauge, hex keys, and a printed
  copy of the measurements from the Setup Standard.

## Notes

- Fretwork does not sell pedals, cables, or anything it does not use on the
  bench.
- UK accessories shipping is £4.95, free over £75, next working day if ordered
  before 14:00.
- Opened and used accessories are not returnable unless faulty; the 30-day
  play-it-at-home policy covers instruments.
