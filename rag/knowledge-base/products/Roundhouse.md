# Roundhouse

Fretwork's amplifier, and the only product not built by the two instrument
workshops. Introduced in 2022 as the companion to the Meridian.

## Specifications

- 15-watt valve combo; two EL84 power valves, three ECC83 preamp valves.
- Single 12" speaker.
- Two channels (clean and drive), shared three-band EQ, spring reverb.
- Half-power switch (15W / 7W) and a line out for recording.

## Pricing and options

- Roundhouse: £749.
- Covering: Black, Oxblood.
- Footswitch for channel and reverb: +£45.

## Notes

- Fretwork does not sell pedals or a modelling amp; the Roundhouse is the whole
  amplifier range.
- Sold in a Meridian + Roundhouse bundle at £1,499.
- Valves are consumable and are not covered by the two-year warranty; the 30-day
  play-it-at-home policy still applies.
