# Lowtide

Fretwork's bass line, added in 2021 after Marcus Reyes argued the company should
sell a bass worth setting up rather than keep repairing cheap ones.

## Specifications

- Ash body, bolt-on maple neck, 34" scale.
- Available as Lowtide 4 (four string) and Lowtide 5 (five string, 35" scale).
- Passive split-coil pickup plus a bridge single coil; volume, blend, tone.
- Heavy-mass bridge, open-gear tuners.

## Pricing and options

- Lowtide 4: £949. Lowtide 5: £1,049.
- Finishes: Sea Foam, Midnight, Natural.
- Ships in a fitted gig bag; hard case +£140.

## Notes

- Set up with .045-.105 strings as standard.
- The Lowtide 5 is built to order only; add one week to the build-to-ship date.
- Covered by the 30-day play-it-at-home returns policy.
