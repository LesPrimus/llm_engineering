# Tomas Lindqvist

## Role

Warehouse Manager. Joined September 2021; runs the Bristol warehouse, packing,
and everything that happens to an order after the bench signs it off.

## Profile

- Based in Bristol; on site every day, because the warehouse is.
- Came from a shipping company rather than from music, and says the instruments
  are the least fragile thing they have ever packed given how they are boxed.
- Wrote the packing spec: case inside a double-walled carton, neck blocked,
  strings slackened a tone, humidity pack for acoustics in winter.
- Owns the courier contracts and the free-return label flow with Sofia Delgado.

## Notes

- Cut in-transit damage from roughly one order in forty to one in three hundred
  in their first year, mostly by changing the carton rather than the courier.
- Keeps the bench queue and the packing bench physically separate so a rushed
  order cannot skip a setup.
- Pushes every year for EU warehousing, on the grounds that an EU return
  currently crosses the border twice.
