# Marcus Reyes

## Role

Head of Guitar Tech. Joined August 2019 (employee number four) and runs the
setup bench in Bristol.

## Profile

- Luthier by training; twelve years repairing instruments before Fretwork.
- Every guitar Fretwork ships passes through his team: fret level check, nut
  slot check, action and intonation set to the customer's chosen string gauge.
- Writes the setup card that ships in the case of each instrument.

## Notes

- Owns the Fretwork Setup Standard, the checklist behind the "plays right out of
  the box" claim on the storefront.
- Trained the three-person bench team and refuses to grow it faster than he can
  train it, which is the usual reason ship dates slip.
- The Lowtide bass exists because he kept rebuilding cheap basses for customers
  and argued Fretwork should just sell a good one.
