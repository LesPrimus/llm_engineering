# Amara Nwosu

## Role

Senior Guitar Tech. Joined March 2022 as the second person on the setup bench,
and now leads the three-person bench team day to day under Marcus Reyes.

## Profile

- Based in Bristol, at the bench.
- Trained as a violin repairer, which is where the fret-levelling technique the
  bench now uses came from.
- Does the fret work on every acoustic; Kestrel Lutherie's necks arrive closer
  to finished than Halverson's, and the two need different handling.
- Runs the 90-day free re-setup appointments for customers who come to Bristol.

## Notes

- Trains new bench hires, and is the reason Marcus Reyes can hold the rule that
  the team grows only as fast as it can be trained.
- Keeps the bench log: every instrument, every measurement, every rejection back
  to a workshop. It is the evidence behind the quarterly supplier review.
- Argued for the Halyard's rolled fingerboard edges after setting up the first
  prototypes.
