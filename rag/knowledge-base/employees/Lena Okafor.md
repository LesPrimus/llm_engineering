# Lena Okafor

## Role

Founder and CEO, Fretwork. Joined at founding (March 2019) and still signs off
on every new product line personally.

## Profile

- Based in Bristol, UK; works from the Bristol warehouse two days a week.
- Played bass in a touring band for six years before starting Fretwork, which is
  where the "buy an instrument that has already been set up properly" idea came
  from.
- Owns the relationship with the two workshops that build the Meridian and
  Driftwood lines.

## Notes

- Runs the Monday all-hands, where the week's return rate is the first slide.
- Hired Marcus Reyes as employee number four and treats the setup bench as the
  part of the company that cannot be outsourced.
- Publicly committed to keeping every Fretwork instrument under a two-week
  build-to-ship window; this is the metric she is measured on by the board.
