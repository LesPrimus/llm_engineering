# Priya Raghunathan

## Role

Director of Engineering. Joined January 2021 to replace the off-the-shelf
storefront with something Fretwork owns.

## Profile

- Based in Manchester, UK; the engineering team of six is fully remote.
- Built the current storefront, the stock system that keeps the Bristol
  warehouse and the two workshops in sync, and the returns tooling.
- On call rotation alongside her team; she takes the Christmas week herself.

## Notes

- Her stock system is the reason the storefront can promise a build-to-ship date
  per instrument rather than a generic "ships in 2-4 weeks".
- Pushed for the guided recommender on the storefront after seeing how many
  support tickets were really "which guitar should I buy" questions.
- Keeps a hard rule that no customer-facing page may depend on a third-party
  service that Fretwork cannot run without.
