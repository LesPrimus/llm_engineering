# Jun Watanabe

## Role

Product Designer. Joined June 2020, in time to draw the Lowtide; owns the
drawings, specifications, and tolerances for every Fretwork line.

## Profile

- Based in Bristol, but on the road most months: each of the three partner
  workshops gets a visit.
- Draws in CAD and prototypes in wood, and keeps every rejected prototype on a
  rack in the warehouse.
- Sets the tolerances the workshops build to, and decides with Amara Nwosu
  whether an out-of-tolerance instrument is benched or sent back.
- Designed the Skylark around a single question: what does a guitar look like if
  it has to be comfortable to play sitting on a bed.

## Notes

- Works from the bench log rather than from customer surveys, on the argument
  that what a setup has to correct is the design's fault.
- The baritone Meridian on the roadmap is currently with them for a second round
  of neck-profile changes.
- Holds a standing veto on any change that would make an instrument harder to
  set up.
