# Roadmap

What Fretwork has committed to next, as agreed at the last board meeting. Not
published on the storefront.

## Next twelve months

- A baritone Meridian, prototyped at Halverson Works and sitting with Jun
  Watanabe for a second round of neck-profile changes.
- Skylark E stock built ahead of order for the first time, to test whether a
  ready-to-ship acoustic dents the return rate.
- Setup-card archive opened to customers by serial number, whether or not they
  bought the instrument new — Priya Raghunathan's team, second half of the year.
- EU warehousing, so EU orders stop crossing the border twice on a return.

## Explicitly not doing

- Pedals and modelling amps. The Roundhouse remains the whole amplifier range.
- Selling through dealers. Every instrument leaves Bristol set up, and Fretwork
  will not ship one it has not set up.
- A seven-string or an eight-string; the volume does not carry a separate
  workshop run.
- Dual-sourcing a line, for now.

## Constraints

- Bench capacity is the binding constraint on every line item here, and it grows
  only as fast as the bench team is trained.
- The two-week build-to-ship window is the metric Lena Okafor is measured on by
  the board, and no roadmap item is allowed to widen it.
