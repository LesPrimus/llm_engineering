# Careers

Fretwork is 27 people: the Bristol warehouse and setup bench, a remote
engineering team, and support.

## How the company is set up

- Bench and warehouse are in Bristol and on site, because the work is physical.
- Engineering is fully remote, six people, hired anywhere in UK or EU time.
- Support is four people covering email and live chat, 09:00-18:00 UK time,
  Monday to Saturday.

## Hiring

- Bench roles are hired on a trade test: level and crown a fret on a scrap neck,
  cut a nut slot, set an action to a given spec.
- Engineering interviews are a paid day working on a real ticket, no whiteboard.
- Fretwork has never hired a salesperson, and the storefront has no upsell flow.

## What Fretwork offers

- Salary bands published internally and reviewed each March.
- Four-day week for the bench team over July and August, when order volume dips.
- One instrument at cost per employee per year, set up by the bench.
- Training budget for bench staff to spend on lutherie courses; Marcus Reyes
  approves and usually insists it is spent.

## Notes

- The bench team grows only as fast as Marcus Reyes can train it, which is a
  standing constraint on how many instruments Fretwork can ship in a week.
- Every new hire, whatever the role, spends their first day on the bench
  watching a setup end to end.
