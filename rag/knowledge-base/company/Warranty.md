# Warranty

Every Fretwork instrument and amplifier carries a two-year warranty from the
date of delivery, on top of the 30-day play-it-at-home returns policy.

## What is covered

- Manufacturing and materials faults: neck joint, bracing, finish flaws, fret
  seating, hardware and electronics failure in ordinary use.
- The Roundhouse chassis, transformers, speaker, and switching.
- Labour and return shipping both ways for a warranty repair.

## What is not covered

- Consumables: strings, valves, batteries, and fret wear.
- Setup drift — relief, action, and intonation move with humidity and season.
  This is a free re-setup within 90 days, not a warranty claim.
- Damage from an accident, from a modification, or from a repair done outside
  Fretwork.
- Second-hand instruments; the warranty is not transferable.

## How a claim runs

1. Support takes photographs and the serial number from the setup card.
2. Marcus Reyes or a bench tech decides repair, replace, or refund.
3. Repairs are done in Bristol where possible, and at the building workshop
   where they are structural.

## Notes

- The two-year term was set deliberately longer than the one year the two
  workshops offer Fretwork, and the difference is carried by Fretwork.
- Warranty claims run at roughly 1.5% of instruments shipped, and are reported
  quarterly by Ruth Feldman alongside the return rate.
