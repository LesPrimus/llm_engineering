# Shipping

How a Fretwork order gets from the Bristol warehouse to a customer. Owned by
Tomas Lindqvist, with the promised dates coming from Priya Raghunathan's stock
system.

## Instruments and amplifiers

- Built to order. The storefront quotes a build-to-ship date per instrument at
  checkout; the company target is two weeks.
- UK delivery is free, by insured two-day courier, signature required.
- EU and Norway: £75, delivered duty paid, four to seven working days.
- Rest of world: quoted at checkout; duties and import taxes are the customer's.

## Accessories

- UK: £4.95, or free over £75, next working day if ordered before 14:00.
- Shipped from stock rather than built, so an accessories-only order does not
  wait on the bench queue.

## Packing

- Every instrument ships in its case or gig bag, inside a double-walled carton
  with the neck blocked and the strings slackened a tone.
- A humidity pack goes in with every acoustic between November and March.

## Notes

- Orders mixing an instrument and accessories ship together on the instrument's
  date unless the customer asks for a split shipment, which is free.
- Fretwork does not ship to a forwarding address; a return needs to come back
  from the address the instrument went to.
