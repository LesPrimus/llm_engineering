# Fretwork

Fretwork is an online guitar store founded in Bristol, UK in March 2019 by Lena
Okafor. It sells four own-brand instruments and amplifiers, built by two partner
workshops and set up in-house before shipping.

## What makes it different

Every instrument is set up on the Bristol bench before it ships — fret level,
nut slots, action, and intonation dialled in for the string gauge the customer
picks at checkout. That is the Fretwork Setup Standard, owned by Marcus Reyes,
and it is why the storefront claims an instrument "plays right out of the box".

## Numbers

- 27 employees; warehouse and setup bench in Bristol, engineering remote.
- Four product lines: Meridian (electric), Driftwood (acoustic), Lowtide (bass),
  Roundhouse (amplifier).
- Build-to-ship target of two weeks per instrument, quoted per order at
  checkout.
- 30-day play-it-at-home returns, shipping paid by Fretwork.
