# Trade-in Programme

Fretwork takes a customer's first instrument in part-exchange against a new one.
Run by Sofia Delgado since 2023.

## How it works

1. The customer sends photographs and the model through the storefront.
2. Support quotes a trade-in value, held for 21 days.
3. The old instrument ships to Bristol on a Fretwork-paid label, and the value
   comes off the new order.
4. If the instrument arrives materially worse than described, the customer
   chooses between a revised quote and having it sent back, free.

## What is taken

- Any electric, acoustic, or bass in playable condition, Fretwork-made or not.
- Not amplifiers, not pedals, not instruments with structural damage.
- Typical values run £80 to £250; the ceiling is £400 and needs Ruth Feldman's
  sign-off.

## What happens to it

- Traded instruments go over the bench, then to a local music charity that
  teaches in Bristol schools; Fretwork does not resell them.
- Instruments too far gone are stripped for hardware, which the bench uses for
  repairs.

## Notes

- The programme is deliberately break-even. It exists because a customer
  upgrading from a starter guitar is the exact person the Meridian was designed
  for, and because giving the old one away is easier to explain than reselling
  it.
