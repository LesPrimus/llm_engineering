# Returns Policy

The 30-day play-it-at-home policy, written and owned by Sofia Delgado. It covers
every instrument and amplifier Fretwork sells.

## The policy

- Any instrument can be returned within 30 days of delivery, for any reason,
  including "it isn't the guitar I wanted".
- Return shipping is paid by Fretwork: support emails a printed label.
- The instrument must come back in the case or gig bag it shipped in. This is
  the one hard condition.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within five working days of
  the instrument arriving back in Bristol.

## What is not covered

- Damage beyond ordinary playing wear — a dropped instrument, a cracked top, a
  bent tuner post.
- Left-handed and other built-to-order options past 30 days; inside 30 days they
  return like any other order.
- Accessories that have been opened and used, other than faulty ones.

## Notes

- A returned instrument goes back over the bench before it is resold, and is
  sold as B-stock at 15% off with the return disclosed.
- The weekly return rate is the first slide at the Monday all-hands.
- Sofia Delgado's standing argument is that a return is a failure of the buying
  advice rather than of the instrument, which is why every return is logged with
  a reason and read at the all-hands.
