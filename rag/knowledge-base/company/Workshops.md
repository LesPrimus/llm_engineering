# Workshops

Fretwork designs and sets up its instruments but does not build them. Three
partner workshops do, and the relationships with them are owned by Lena Okafor
for the two instrument builders and by Ruth Feldman for the contracts.

## Halverson Works — Frome, Somerset

- Builds the solid-body lines: Meridian, Lowtide, and Halyard.
- Fourteen people; Fretwork is roughly 60% of their output, which is the single
  largest supplier risk on the register.
- Bodies and necks are CNC roughed and finished by hand; Fretwork buys them
  complete but unset, without strings.

## Kestrel Lutherie — Totnes, Devon

- Builds the acoustic lines: Driftwood and Skylark.
- Six people; slower and more seasonal than Halverson, and the reason acoustic
  build-to-ship dates run a week longer in winter.
- Holds Fretwork's tonewood stock and seasons it on site for two years.

## Ashcroft Amplification — Cardiff

- Builds the Roundhouse: chassis, transformers, cabinet, and final assembly.
- The only partner not building instruments, and the only one selling under its
  own name as well.

## Notes

- All three workshops give Fretwork a one-year fault warranty; Fretwork's
  customer warranty is two years, and the second year is carried by Fretwork.
- Jun Watanabe holds the drawings and tolerances for every line and visits each
  workshop monthly.
- Fretwork has never dual-sourced a line. The board raises it every year; the
  answer so far is that a second builder would mean two setups to standardise.
