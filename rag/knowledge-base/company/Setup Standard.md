# Setup Standard

The checklist every Fretwork instrument passes before it ships, owned by Marcus
Reyes and carried out on the Bristol bench. It is the whole of the "plays right
out of the box" claim on the storefront.

## The checklist

1. Neck relief set with a straight edge and feeler gauges, to spec for the
   string gauge chosen at checkout.
2. Fret level check; any fret out of plane is levelled, crowned, and polished.
3. Nut slots cut to height and lubricated; a new nut is fitted if the slots are
   cut low.
4. Action set at the 12th fret: 1.6mm bass side, 1.2mm treble side on electrics;
   2.0mm / 1.6mm on acoustics.
5. Intonation set at pitch with the customer's gauge fitted.
6. Electronics tested: every pickup, every switch position, every control
   through the full sweep.
7. Hardware check: tuner tension, strap buttons, truss rod nut, bridge screws.

## What ships with it

- A signed setup card recording the gauge, the relief, and the action measured
  at the end of the check.
- The offcut of the string set used, so a customer can match it.

## Notes

- A guitar that fails the fret level check goes back to the workshop rather than
  onto the bench queue; this is the usual reason an order misses the two-week
  build-to-ship target.
- The standard is deliberately not published in full on the storefront — the
  setup card is the customer-facing version of it.
- Setup drifts with humidity and season. A free re-setup is offered within the
  first 90 days for UK customers who bring an instrument back to Bristol.
